Our Mission

Chess on the Street is dedicated to bringing the transformative power of chess to underserved communities worldwide. We believe that chess is more than a game—it's a tool for developing critical thinking, strategic planning, and building stronger, more resilient communities.

Our Programs

Youth Chess Education

Free chess lessons and mentorship for young people in underserved communities.

Community Chess Clubs

Establishing local chess clubs that serve as community hubs for learning and connection.

Tournament Support

Organizing and sponsoring chess tournaments to showcase talent and build confidence.

Mentorship Programs

Connecting youth with experienced chess players and life mentors.
